# GrowthBridge Brand Guidelines

## Brand architecture
- **Parent organization:** GrowthBridge
- **Public-facing brand and digital platform:** GrowthBridge Virtual
- Use **GrowthBridge Virtual** in public-facing website, campaign and customer communications. Use **GrowthBridge** when referring to the parent organization or organizational ownership.
- Do not present GrowthBridge Virtual as a separate legal entity unless the organization formally establishes that status.

## Brand promise and tagline
**Tagline:** Bridging Skills. Driving Growth.

**Positioning:** A connected growth and innovation ecosystem that brings people, businesses, technology and communities together to turn potential into practical opportunity.

**Personality:** Innovative, ambitious, trustworthy, inclusive, practical and community-minded.

**Voice:** Clear, confident, encouraging and professional. Prefer plain language, specific outcomes and active verbs. Avoid inflated claims, jargon and promises that cannot be evidenced.

## Logo system
- Primary lockup: bridge symbol + GrowthBridge wordmark + Virtual descriptor.
- Secondary lockup: bridge symbol + GrowthBridge wordmark.
- Standalone mark: bridge symbol only, for favicons, avatars and compact placements.
- Use the supplied SVG assets in `public/brand/` and `public/icons/`.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the height of the central pillar.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour individual components, add effects, or place the logo on a low-contrast background.
- On dark backgrounds use the supplied light wordmark treatment or a white wordmark; preserve the official mark colours.

## Official core colours
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Bridge Navy | `#123B5D` |
| Growth | Growth Green | `#16A36A` |
| Accent | Opportunity Gold | `#F59E0B` |
| Light surface | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Navy communicates trust and stability; green signals growth and progress; gold highlights opportunity and calls to action. Derived shades may be used for accessibility, states and dark-mode contrast, but must not replace the core identity colours.

**Accessibility:** use navy or another tested dark foreground on Growth Green for normal-size text. Do not use white text on the core green for small text. Check all combinations against WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text and meaningful UI graphics).

## Typography
- Display and headings: Montserrat, weight 600–800.
- Body and interface: Montserrat, weight 400–600.
- Use a clear hierarchy, generous line-height and short readable paragraphs.
- Use a system sans-serif fallback if Montserrat is unavailable. Do not introduce decorative display fonts.

## Website and digital experience
- Public site defaults to light mode; dark mode is an explicit visitor choice.
- Admin Control Center defaults to dark mode; its preference remains independent.
- Use navy for trust, structure and major headings; green for primary actions and progress; gold sparingly for highlights and opportunity cues.
- Preserve the established navigation, page architecture, CMS, service catalogue, talent, projects, events and integration workflows.
- Photography should be authentic, inclusive, people-centred and relevant to the service being described. Avoid generic stock imagery where a real project or community story is available.
- Motion should be purposeful, subtle, reduced when the user requests reduced motion, and never required to understand content.

## Organization divisions
Use these names and descriptions consistently:
- **GrowthBridge Digital** — web and mobile development, product design, e-commerce, APIs, cloud and AI solutions.
- **GrowthBridge Business** — brand strategy, market research, digital marketing, business consulting and growth planning.
- **GrowthBridge People** — talent sourcing, mentorship, career development, internships and leadership development.
- **GrowthBridge Community** — digital literacy, youth bootcamps, community technology hubs and social-impact initiatives.
- **GrowthBridge Events** — conferences, hackathons, workshops, networking and virtual or hybrid events.

## Social media system
- Avatar: standalone bridge mark on navy.
- Cover/banner: navy foundation, green growth motif, gold accent, wordmark and tagline; keep key text within platform safe areas.
- Post template: one clear headline, one supporting message, one action, consistent logo placement and strong contrast.
- Suggested content pillars: Digital Innovation, Business Growth, People & Talent, Community Impact, Events & Opportunities.
- Keep copy accessible and useful; add captions/alt text to visual content and do not place essential information only inside an image.

## Messaging framework
**Short description:** GrowthBridge Virtual connects people, businesses and communities with digital solutions, talent, learning and opportunities.

**Homepage headline direction:** Connect potential to opportunity.

**Supporting line:** Discover digital solutions, business support, talent pathways, community programmes and events built to help people and organizations grow.

**Draft mission (for leadership review):** To connect people, businesses and communities with technology, talent and opportunities that support meaningful growth.

**Draft vision (for leadership review):** A connected ecosystem where people and organizations can turn potential into sustainable progress.

Mission and vision wording above is proposed brand copy, not a formally adopted governance statement.

## Governance
- This guide is the working visual and verbal source of truth for GrowthBridge Virtual.
- Changes to core colours, logo geometry, parent/public brand hierarchy or mission/vision require organization-owner approval.
- Product, service and impact claims must be checked against actual delivery capability and evidence before publication.
